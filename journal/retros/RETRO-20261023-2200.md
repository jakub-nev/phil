## Lesson

No strategy edit. This is the second category (countable-metric, after
weather) where a one-sided book with only a bid inflates brier_delta on a
fact-final mechanical read. The playbook's no-book skip logic is already
correct here. Flagging again so a future deep retro excludes one-sided-book
rows from category brier_delta verdicts across categories, not just weather
— two independent instances now point the same way.
