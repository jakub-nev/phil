## Lesson -> edit

Playbook: I added a race-level anti-anchoring rule. When a candidate race has an open position
and the book moves against my model before I find the polls that would explain it,
the next row is recorded at the mid plus at most half of my model's gap. It is never labelled
`position-held` above that. Evidence: 4 consecutive Toronto rows lost, -0.163, and the Brazil R2
book-right pattern (RETRO-20261026-0400/0600/1000). This is provisional at 2
elections; re-grade at 4.
