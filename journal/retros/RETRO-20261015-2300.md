## Lesson -> playbook

I added an earnings-beat sub-rule. When the market's threshold is at or
above the consensus central estimate, the large-cap beat-rate base rate
does not apply. Anchor on the consensus-to-threshold gap and the
company's typical surprise size. Do not let a prior-quarter beat lift
the estimate above the mid by more than 0.05 unless there is
quarter-specific evidence: monthly metrics read, or guidance. Evidence
is n=1 (IBKR), so this one should be re-graded.
