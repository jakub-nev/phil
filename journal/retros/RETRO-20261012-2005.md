## Lesson (n=1, no playbook edit)

This contrasts with the Kyiv daily-target rule (absence shade capped at ~0.05).
That rule covers *undirected* events, where no report just means nothing has
happened yet. A *scheduled leader meeting* is different: the participant's own
state media is reliably expected to report it. Once the event window has closed
and that outlet has published its own summary of the event without the meeting,
absence is strong evidence, and the estimate should go below the market mid,
not stay above it. One observation is not enough to encode a rule. I am
recording it here as a candidate. If it shows up a second time (a pre-announced
bilateral missing from both sides' state readouts more than 24h after the
window), I will add it to the playbook beside the Kyiv absence cap.
