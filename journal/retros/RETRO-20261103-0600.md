The ladders are BanRep Sep, RBI Oct, BoI Oct 21, BoC Oct 28 and RBA Nov 3.
In all 5, the rows that shaded the modal leg below the mid (or put the
minority leg above its mid) lost to the market. The at-mid rows matched or
beat it. 5 of 5 with the same sign: the rule stands, and it stays
forecast-only.

Two additions, encoded in the playbook:
1. Secondary reporting of futures/OIS odds that is older than the live PM
   book does not count as a "numeric survey" and does not license a
   below-mid modal record. When the PM mid is above the reported futures
   odds, the PM price is the fresher read.
2. A sibling-consistency recompute must not push the modal leg below its
   own mid. If the complement arithmetic lands below the mid, record the
   modal leg at its mid and let the overround stay on the minority legs.
