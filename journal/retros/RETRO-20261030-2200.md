## Lesson -> edit

**Playbook, modal-leg rule (central-bank ladders):** the numeric-survey
exception does not apply when the most recent decision was a split vote
with the majority margin at one vote, or when the survey consensus missed
the previous meeting's outcome. In that case the modal and consensus legs
stay within 0.10 of the mid, and the survey read goes in the note as
`model view:`. Evidence: BanRep Oct 17 row 0.42 vs 0.67, +0.228 dBrier;
July and Sep 30 BanRep surveys also missed. This is narrow on purpose. It
covers 1 graded row and 3 meetings of one bank, and it exempts CBR and RBI,
where the numeric-poll rows won.
